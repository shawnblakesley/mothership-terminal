          How to play
          
      
    
          
    
          
      
          Mothership 1e at a glance. The full rules are in the Player's Survival Guide (Tuesday Knight Games · mothershiprpg.com ↗).
          
      
          Mothership is survival horror. You are workers, not heroes: stay alive, find out what happened, and get out. Running, hiding and bargaining are good plans.
          

      
          Rolling
          
      
            
        
          • When the Warden needs a roll, a ROLL REQUIRED box appears. Roll on screen, or roll real dice and type the number in.
            • 
        
          • Roll d100 (00–99). Under your number succeeds; equal or over fails.
            • 
        
          • Your number is a Stat or Save from your crew file, + a Skill if one fits: Trained +10, Expert +15, Master +20.
            • 
        
          • Doubles (00, 11 … 99) are critical: a critical success, or a critical failure.
            • 
        
          • [+] Advantage: roll twice, keep the better. [-] Disadvantage: keep the worse.
            • 
        
          • Every failed roll adds 1 Stress.
            • 
      
          

      
          Stats and saves
          
      
          
        
          Strength
          lifting, pushing, holding on, brute force
          
        
          Speed
          getting there first, dodging, reacting
          
        
          Intellect
          knowing, fixing, hacking, working it out
          
        
          Combat
          hitting what you aim at
          
        
          Sanity save
          keeping your grip when things can't be real
          
        
          Fear save
          holding your nerve: terror, gore, being hunted
          
        
          Body save
          enduring toxins, disease, cold, hunger, being thrown about
          
      
          

      
          Stress and Panic
          
      
            
        
          • Stress starts at 2 and climbs with every failure and every horror.
            • 
        
          • When something truly awful happens, the Warden may call a Panic check: roll d20. Above your Stress, you hold it together. Equal or under, you panic, and the Warden tells you what that means.
            • 
        
          • Rest somewhere safe to shed Stress.
            • 
      
          

      
          Getting hurt
          
      
            
        
          • Damage comes off your Health. Armour reduces each hit.
            • 
        
          • At 0 Health you take a Wound, then your Health resets. Run out of Wounds and you die.
            • 
      
          

      
          Staying alive
          
      
            
        
          • Ask the terminal things. Information keeps you alive.
            • 
        
          • Say exactly what you do. A good plan may not need a roll at all.
            • 
        
          • Use your gear, your skills and each other.
            • 
        
          • If something feels wrong, it is.
            • 
      
          
      
          Your Warden has the final word.
