            Mothership 1e at a glance. The full rules are in the Player's Survival Guide and Warden's Operations Manual (Tuesday Knight Games · mothershiprpg.com ↗).

            Checks

            • Roll d100 (00–99). Under the target succeeds; equal or over fails.
            • Target = a Stat or Save + a Skill that fits: Trained +10, Expert +15, Master +20.
            • Doubles (00, 11 … 99) are criticals: a critical success or a critical failure.
            • [+] Advantage / [-] Disadvantage: roll twice, keep the better / worse.
            • Any failed check or save: +1 Stress.
            • Only roll when failing would be interesting and the outcome is uncertain. Otherwise it just happens (or just doesn't).
            Strength
            lifting, pushing, holding on, brute force
            Speed
            getting there first, dodging, reacting
            Intellect
            knowing, fixing, hacking, working it out
            Combat
            hitting what you aim at
            Sanity save
            keeping a rational grip: hallucinations, things that can't be real
            Fear save
            holding your nerve: terror, gore, being hunted
            Body save
            enduring: toxins, disease, cold, hunger, being thrown about

            Stress & Panic

            • Stress starts at 2 and climbs with every failure and every horror.
            • Panic check: roll d20. Above current Stress, they hold it together. Equal or under, they Panic: look the roll up on the Panic Table.
            • Call one when something truly awful happens: a crewmate dies, the thing is in the room, the lights go out for good.
            • Rest somewhere safe (as you allow) to shed Stress.

            Harm

            • Damage comes off Health. Armour (AP) reduces each hit.
            • At 0 Health they take a Wound: roll on the Wound Table for the kind of harm (blunt, bleeding, gunshot, fire, gore), then Health resets.
            • When Wounds reach their maximum, the character dies (or is dying, as you rule).

            Combat

            • Describe the threat; everyone says what they do; resolve it in the order that makes sense.
            • Attacking: a Combat check. A hit deals the weapon's damage.

            Running it

            • Warn before danger: show the blood trail before the creature.
            • Say the stakes before the roll: what happens if it fails.
            • Failure moves the story: something gets worse, costs time, or makes noise. It rarely means "nothing happens".
            • They are workers, not heroes. Running, hiding and bargaining are good plans.

            In this app

            • Actions → Roll calls a check, save or Panic check for one character or everyone; each rolls on their own screen against their own sheet, and failures add Stress by themselves.
            • ⚖ Your call means the agent saw an uncertain attempt and is waiting for you: it shows the stakes (what happens if it works, and if it fails), then you say it works, it fails, or roll. Tell the players the stakes before they roll; the agent narrates by them.
            • The agent can change Health, Wounds and Stress (Settings decides); you can always fix them on the Crew tab.
